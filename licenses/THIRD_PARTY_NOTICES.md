DiffLinker source is distributed under the MIT license in `DiffLinker-MIT.txt` and `vendor/DiffLinker/LICENSE`. The source revision is recorded in `vendor/DiffLinker/UPSTREAM_COMMIT`.

The pretrained model was published in [DiffLinker Models](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10988017) under CC BY 4.0. The kinase checkpoint derives from that model. The distributed checkpoints preserve all network tensors and replace runtime path metadata with relative paths. See [the DiffLinker article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-024-00815-9).

The synthetic accessibility scorer retains its BSD notice in `SA-score-BSD.txt`. Structural identifiers and KLIFS annotations identify the public sources. Bibliographic citations are listed in `references.bib`.
